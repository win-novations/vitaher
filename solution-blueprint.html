Executive Summary

ABC Services operates a geographically distributed service delivery network spanning more than 25 operational centres, each with its own quality processes, compliance obligations, and improvement cadence. Today, quality management across this network relies on fragmented manual processes: spreadsheet-based audit tracking, email-driven corrective actions, siloed document repositories, and regional reporting that reaches the executive layer weeks after the events it describes.

This blueprint defines a unified Enterprise Quality Platform that replaces those fragmented practices with a single, intelligent operating environment. The platform is built entirely within the Microsoft 365 ecosystem — SharePoint Online, Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI, AI Builder, and Microsoft Copilot — so that it inherits the organisation's existing identity, security, and collaboration infrastructure without introducing new vendor dependencies.

The platform is organised around 17 integrated modules that collectively cover every clause of ISO 9001:2015, from document control and internal audits through to management review and continual improvement. It serves seven distinct user personas across three organisational tiers — Executive, Regional, and Site — each with role-appropriate dashboards, workflows, and decision-support tools.

The data model centres on Dataverse as the transactional store, with SharePoint Online providing document lifecycle management. Row-Level Security. Every transactional entity carries a Centre lookup field. Dataverse business units map one-to-one to operational centres. Centre Managers see only their centre's data; Regional Managers see all centres within their region; Executives see the full enterprise dataset. This is enforced at the platform layer, not the application layer.

Integration Architecture

The platform integrates across three boundaries: internal module-to-module, Microsoft 365 ecosystem, and external systems.

Internal module integration

All modules share the Dataverse schema. Cross-module workflows are implemented as Power Automate cloud flows triggered by Dataverse record changes. For example, closing an Audit Finding with a severity of Major automatically creates a Non-Conformance record, which in turn triggers the CAPA workflow. These integrations are event-driven, not batch.

04 End-to-End User Journeys

Six primary journeys illustrate how users interact with the platform across modules. Each journey traces a quality event from its origin through resolution, showing the actors, system actions, and decision points involved.

Non-Conformance through Corrective Action Closure ISO 10.2
1
NC Raised. A Centre Manager identifies a process deviation during daily operations and creates a Non-Conformance record via the quick-create button on their dashboard. They classify it as a Minor NC, describe the deviation, upload a photo as evidence, and record the immediate containment action taken.
Actor: Centre Manager
2
Auto-Classification & Notification. The system validates the NC, assigns a unique reference number (NC-2026-0847), and triggers a notification to the Regional Quality Manager. AI Builder analyses the description and suggests a category and ISO clause mapping, which the Centre Manager confirms or overrides.
System: Power Automate + AI Builder
3
Root Cause Analysis. The Centre Manager uses the guided 5 Whys form to investigate the root cause. They document each "why" step, attach supporting evidence, and identify the root cause as a training gap in a recently updated SOP.
Actor: Centre Manager
4
CAPA Creation. Because the root cause is systemic (training gap affecting multiple staff), the Centre Manager escalates to a CAPA. The system creates a linked CAPA record pre-populated with the NC details and root cause. A multi-step action plan is defined: revise the SOP, deliver retraining, update the competency assessment.
Actor: Centre Manager → System
5
CAPA Approval. The CAPA plan is routed to the Regional Quality Manager for approval. They review the proposed actions, adjust the timeline for the retraining, and approve. The system notifies all action owners of their assignments and due dates.
Actor: Regional Quality Manager
6
Implementation & Verification. Each action owner completes their tasks and uploads evidence. The Process Owner revises the SOP (triggering the Document Control review workflow). The Centre Manager delivers retraining and records completion in the Training module. As each action is marked complete, the CAPA progress bar updates automatically.
Actors: Process Owner, Centre Manager, Action Owners
7
Effectiveness Review. At 60 days post-implementation, the system triggers an effectiveness review task. The Regional Quality Manager verifies that no similar NCs have occurred since the CAPA was implemented, reviews the retraining records, and marks the CAPA as Effective. The linked NC is closed. The entire chain (NC → RCA → CAPA → Actions → Effectiveness) is preserved as an auditable record.
Actor: Regional Quality Manager → System
Internal Audit Lifecycle ISO 9.2
1
Annual Programme. The Head of Quality builds the annual audit programme using the clause coverage matrix. AI recommends increasing audit frequency at three centres based on rising NC trends. The programme is approved and published.
Actor: Head of Quality
2
Audit Scheduling. The Quality Administrator creates individual audit schedules from the programme, assigns auditors (with independence validation), and sets dates. Outlook calendar invitations are sent automatically to auditors and auditees.
Actor: Quality Administrator → System
3
Preparation. The assigned Internal Auditor opens the audit record, reviews the checklist (mapped to ISO clauses and centre-specific requirements), and downloads relevant SOPs and previous audit reports. They prepare the audit plan and notify the Centre Manager.
Actor: Internal Auditor
4
On-Site Execution. The Auditor uses the Canvas App on their tablet. For each checklist item, they record the finding (Conforming, Observation, Minor NC, Major NC), add notes, and capture photo evidence. GPS tagging confirms they are on-site. Offline mode allows data capture in areas with poor connectivity, syncing when back online.
Actor: Internal Auditor (mobile)
5
Reporting & Findings. The Auditor completes the audit report with an executive summary and detailed findings. Major and Minor findings automatically generate Non-Conformance records linked to the audit. The Centre Manager receives the report and must accept or challenge each finding within 5 business days.
Actor: Internal Auditor → System → Centre Manager
6
Follow-Up. The Auditor's dashboard shows open findings from previous audits at the same centre. During the next scheduled audit, they verify whether corrective actions from prior findings have been implemented and are effective, closing the follow-up loop.
Actor: Internal Auditor
Customer Complaint Resolution ISO 9.1.2
1
Intake. A customer complaint arrives via email. Power Automate parses the email, creates a Complaint record with the extracted details, and AI Builder classifies the complaint type (Service Delivery — Timeliness) and assigns it to the relevant centre.
System: Power Automate + AI Builder
2
Acknowledgement. An automated acknowledgement email is sent to the customer within 2 hours, including a reference number and expected response timeline. The Centre Manager is notified via Teams.
System: Power Automate
3
Investigation. The Centre Manager investigates the complaint, documents findings, and determines whether the complaint is valid. If valid and the severity warrants it, they create a linked Non-Conformance. All customer communications are logged against the complaint record.
Actor: Centre Manager
4
Resolution & Follow-Up. The Centre Manager records the resolution, closes the complaint, and the system sends a resolution notification to the customer. Seven days later, a satisfaction survey is triggered automatically via Microsoft Forms. The survey response is recorded against the complaint.
Actor: Centre Manager → System
Document Lifecycle ISO 7.5
1
Authoring. A Process Owner creates a new SOP using a standardised template from the template library. They complete the metadata (process area, applicable centres, competency requirements) and write the procedural content.
Actor: Process Owner
2
Review & Approval. The Process Owner submits the document for review. The workflow routes it to designated reviewers (subject-matter experts) for comment, then to the Regional Quality Manager for approval. Reviewers can annotate directly in SharePoint. All comments are tracked and resolved before final approval.
Actors: Reviewers, Regional Quality Manager
3
Distribution & Acknowledgement. On approval, the document is published to the controlled document library. All personnel at applicable centres receive a notification with a link to the document and a requirement to acknowledge they have read and understood it. The system tracks acknowledgement completion rates and sends reminders to non-respondents.
System: Power Automate → All affected personnel
4
Periodic Review. The system schedules the document for review based on its review period (e.g., 12 months). Thirty days before the review date, the Process Owner is notified. They either confirm the document is still current (extending the review date) or initiate a revision, restarting the lifecycle from Step 1. If no action is taken, the document status changes to "Review Overdue" and escalates to the Regional Quality Manager.
System → Actor: Process Owner
Risk Identification to Treatment ISO 6.1
1
Risk Identification. During a quarterly risk review, a Centre Manager identifies a new risk: a key supplier has shown declining delivery performance over three consecutive months. They create a Risk record, categorise it (Supply Chain — Reliability), and link it to the relevant supplier in the Supplier Quality module.
Actor: Centre Manager
2
Assessment. The Centre Manager assesses the risk using the 5×5 matrix: Likelihood = 4 (Likely), Impact = 3 (Moderate). The system calculates an inherent risk score of 12 (High) and colour-codes the risk on the centre heat map.
Actor: Centre Manager
3
Treatment Planning. The Centre Manager defines a mitigation plan: qualify an alternative supplier within 60 days and renegotiate delivery terms with the current supplier. The treatment plan is submitted to the Regional Quality Manager for approval.
Actor: Centre Manager → Regional Quality Manager
4
Monitoring & Review. The risk is scheduled for monthly review. The AI Insights Engine monitors supplier NC data and flags if the supplier's performance deteriorates further. At each review, the Centre Manager updates the risk status and residual score. Once the alternative supplier is qualified, the residual risk is reassessed to 6 (Medium).
Actor: Centre Manager + System: AI Insights
Management Review Cycle ISO 9.3
1
Input Assembly. Four weeks before the scheduled management review, the system begins assembling review inputs automatically: audit results summary, NC/CAPA statistics and trends, customer complaint analysis, supplier performance overview, objective/KPI status, risk register changes, resource and training compliance, and actions from the previous review. AI Copilot generates a narrative executive summary highlighting the three most significant quality concerns.
System: Power Automate + AI Copilot
2
Review & Preparation. The Head of Quality reviews the assembled inputs, adds commentary, and finalises the agenda. The review meeting invitation is sent to all attendees with the input pack attached. The system validates that all mandatory ISO 9.3.2 inputs are present and flags any gaps.
Actor: Head of Quality
3
Meeting Execution. During the review meeting, the Head of Quality uses the Management Review app to walk through each input area. Decisions are recorded in real time: resource allocations, improvement priorities, system changes, and follow-up actions. Each decision is assigned an owner and due date.
Actor: Head of Quality + Executive attendees
4
Output Distribution & Tracking. The meeting minutes are generated, approved, and distributed as a controlled document. All actions from the review feed into the Action Tracking module. The Quality Administrator monitors action completion and provides a status update at the next review, closing the continual improvement loop.
Actor: Quality Administrator → System

07 AI Strategy

The intelligence layer transforms historical quality data into predictive, prescriptive, and generative capabilities. AI is introduced progressively — beginning with classification and pattern detection, then advancing to prediction and autonomous recommendations as data maturity grows.

Data Maturity Prerequisite. Most predictive and generative AI capabilities require 12–18 months of clean, structured data from the platform's operational modules. The roadmap sequences AI capabilities accordingly: classification and NLP in Phase 1, pattern detection in Phase 2, and predictive models in Phase 3.
Predictive Quality Trend Analysis
Forecasts quality performance metrics (NC rates, audit scores, complaint volumes) 3–6 months ahead using time-series analysis of historical centre data. Alerts leaders when a centre's trajectory suggests future degradation before it manifests in lagging indicators.
Azure ML time-series forecasting on Dataverse export; retrained monthly with latest data
Early intervention prevents quality failures and reduces reactive firefighting by 40–60%
Recurring Non-Conformance Analysis
Clusters NC descriptions using NLP to identify recurring themes that may not be captured by manual categorisation. Detects when apparently unrelated NCs share a common root cause or systemic pattern across centres.
AI Builder text classification + Azure Cognitive Services for semantic clustering
Reveals hidden systemic issues; reduces repeat NCs by targeting true root causes rather than symptoms
Intelligent Audit Scheduling
Recommends audit frequency and scope adjustments based on centre risk profiles, historical performance, and quality trend data. Centres with deteriorating quality indicators are recommended for more frequent or deeper audits; consistently high-performing centres may receive reduced audit frequency.
Rule-based model transitioning to ML as data accumulates; factors: NC rate, complaint volume, previous audit scores, CAPA overdue count, risk register severity
Optimises auditor resource allocation; focuses effort where risk is highest
Complaint Auto-Classification & Routing
Analyses incoming complaint text to automatically classify complaint type, assign severity, identify the responsible centre, and route to the appropriate handler. Reduces manual triage time and ensures consistent classification.
AI Builder document processing model trained on historical complaint data; confidence threshold triggers manual review
Reduces complaint triage time from hours to seconds; improves classification consistency
Risk Prediction Engine
Predicts emerging risks by correlating signals across modules: rising NC trends in a category, supplier performance decline, training gaps, and external factors. Generates early-warning risk advisories before risks materialise as non-conformances.
Multi-variate anomaly detection via Azure ML; ingests data from NCs, complaints, supplier evaluations, and training records
Shifts risk management from reactive to predictive; estimated 30% reduction in preventable quality incidents
Management Review Auto-Summary
Uses Microsoft Copilot to generate a narrative executive summary of all management review inputs. Highlights the three most significant quality concerns, recommends priority actions, and compares current-period performance against prior reviews.
Copilot integration with Power BI semantic model; structured prompts generate consistent summary format
Reduces Head of Quality preparation time from days to hours; ensures no critical data is overlooked
Executive Recommendation Engine
Generates prioritised recommendations for executive action based on the combined intelligence from all AI capabilities. Each recommendation includes a confidence score, supporting evidence, and estimated impact. Recommendations are surfaced on the Executive Dashboard and in the weekly Teams digest.
Copilot-driven synthesis of AI Insights outputs; human-in-the-loop validation by Head of Quality before executive distribution
Transforms data overload into actionable intelligence; ensures executive attention is directed at highest-impact items
Anomaly Detection
Continuously monitors quality metrics across all centres and flags statistically significant deviations from expected patterns. For example: a sudden spike in NCs at a centre, an unusual drop in training compliance, or an abnormal increase in a specific complaint category.
Statistical process control charts (X̄ and R charts) implemented in Power BI with alerting via Power Automate
Catches quality degradation in real time rather than waiting for periodic reviews
Intelligent Clause Prioritisation
Analyses historical audit findings, NCs, and CAPA data by ISO clause to identify which clauses represent the greatest risk of non-conformance. Prioritises audit focus and improvement effort toward the clauses with the weakest organisational performance.
Weighted scoring model: clause finding frequency × average severity × CAPA effectiveness rate × time since last audit
Ensures audit and improvement resources target the highest-risk areas of the QMS
Sentiment Analysis on Customer Feedback
Applies NLP sentiment analysis to customer complaint text, survey responses, and communication logs. Detects emerging dissatisfaction trends even when formal complaint volumes remain stable, providing a leading indicator of customer perception shifts.
Azure Cognitive Services Text Analytics with custom training on domain-specific vocabulary
Provides early warning of customer satisfaction decline; enriches complaint trend analysis beyond volume counts

09 Future Roadmap

The platform is delivered over five years in progressive phases. Each phase builds on the capabilities and data accumulated in previous phases, ensuring AI and analytics capabilities have the data maturity they require.

Year 1
Foundation & Core Operations

Establish the platform infrastructure, deploy core quality modules, and migrate from manual processes. Focus on data capture and process standardisation across all 25+ centres.

  • Q1–Q2: Dataverse schema deployment, security model, business unit configuration per centre. Document Control and SOP Management modules go live. SharePoint document library migration.
  • Q2–Q3: Non-Conformance Management and CAPA modules. Internal Audit Management with mobile Canvas App. Action Tracking module. Core notification workflows.
  • Q3–Q4: Customer Complaints module. Training & Competency module. Risk Management module. Site-level and Regional-level dashboards in Power BI.
  • Q4: Objectives & KPIs module. Management Review module. Executive dashboard (v1). User training and adoption programme for all personas.
Year 2
Intelligence & Integration

Introduce AI capabilities that leverage Year 1 data. Integrate with adjacent business systems and expand automation. Begin building the AI-driven insights layer.

  • Q1–Q2: AI Builder complaint auto-classification. Supplier Quality Management module. Change Management module. Escalation workflow framework.
  • Q2–Q3: Copilot integration for natural-language queries across quality data. Recurring NC pattern detection via NLP clustering. Intelligent audit scheduling (rule-based v1).
  • Q3–Q4: HR system integration for competency data. ERP integration for supplier master data. Advanced Power BI dashboards with drill-through and anomaly flagging. Teams channel integration with weekly digest cards.
  • Q4: Management review auto-summary (Copilot). Sentiment analysis on customer feedback. Executive Analytics module (v2) with AI-generated insights.
Year 3
Advanced Analytics & Prediction

With 18+ months of structured data, deploy predictive AI models and advanced analytics. Shift the organisation from reactive quality management to proactive quality intelligence.

  • Q1–Q2: Predictive quality trend forecasting (Azure ML). Risk prediction engine. Centre health scoring algorithm with predictive degradation warnings.
  • Q2–Q3: Intelligent clause prioritisation. Intelligent audit scheduling (ML v2). Executive recommendation engine with confidence scoring.
  • Q3–Q4: Advanced anomaly detection with statistical process control. Cross-module correlation analysis (e.g., training gaps → NC trends → complaint spikes). Process mining integration to identify process bottlenecks.
  • Q4: Self-service analytics for power users. Custom report builder. API exposure for integration with BI tools outside the Microsoft ecosystem.
Year 4
Ecosystem Expansion

Extend the platform to adjacent quality domains: environmental management (ISO 14001), occupational health and safety (ISO 45001), and industry-specific standards. Build the supplier collaboration portal.

  • Q1–Q2: Multi-standard support — extend the data model to accommodate ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 clause mappings alongside ISO 9001. Unified audit checklists covering multiple standards.
  • Q2–Q3: Supplier collaboration portal — external-facing Power Pages site allowing suppliers to respond to evaluations, submit corrective actions, and upload certificates directly.
  • Q3–Q4: Customer self-service complaint portal. IoT integration for real-time quality data from operational equipment (temperature monitoring, calibration alerts). Digital twin concept for centre quality modelling.
  • Q4: Mobile-first redesign of all Canvas Apps with enhanced offline capabilities. Accessibility audit and WCAG 2.1 AA compliance certification for all platform interfaces.
Year 5
Autonomous Quality Operations

The platform evolves toward autonomous quality management — AI-driven decision support, automated regulatory monitoring, and proactive quality interventions that require minimal human oversight for routine operations.

  • Q1–Q2: Autonomous audit scheduling — AI manages the entire audit programme, dynamically adjusting based on real-time risk signals. Human approval required only for programme changes exceeding defined thresholds.
  • Q2–Q3: Regulatory change monitoring — AI monitors ISO and regulatory updates, assesses impact on the QMS, and recommends document and process changes. Generative AI drafts SOP updates for human review.
  • Q3–Q4: Predictive compliance — the platform predicts which centres are at risk of certification non-conformance 6–12 months ahead and prescribes preventive interventions. Continuous improvement engine that identifies and recommends process optimisations without human prompting.
  • Q4: Full platform maturity assessment. Benchmarking against industry peers. Strategic review of platform ROI and next-generation architecture decisions (e.g., migration considerations for emerging Microsoft capabilities).
Outcome by Year 5. ABC Services operates a fully integrated, AI-augmented Enterprise Quality Platform that has reduced quality incident rates by an estimated 45%, cut management review preparation from weeks to hours, achieved 100% audit programme compliance, and shifted the quality function from a compliance overhead to a strategic business differentiator.
